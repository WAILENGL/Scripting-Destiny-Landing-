Strategic Brief

Summary for Mentor Discussion

Key points to walk through with mentors on why this visual direction wins.

1

Ditch the "Fortune-Teller" Palette

Tian Qi uses reddish-brown chocolate tones (`#1c140e`) reminiscent of traditional geomancy shops. Scripting Destiny shifts to Obsidian Sumi Slate & Champagne Gold, immediately conveying modern intellectual rigor.

2

Kill the Inverted Cream Box

Tian Qi has a prominent cream horizontal banner ("CONTINUE READING"). Our previous waitlist card mirrored that exact visual weight. Replacing it with an architectural hairline-gold card eliminates accidental brand confusion.

3

The Xuan Paper Light Mode Moat

Tian Qi cannot offer a compelling light mode because its design system is rooted in dark lacquer. Our Warm Xuan Rice Paper mode creates an instant, defensible identity that appeals to modern executives and readers.

Suggested Questions to Ask Your Mentors:

  1. Positioning: "Does the Obsidian & Champagne Gold direction feel distinct enough from Tian Qi's dark brown occult dashboard?"
  2. Target Audience: "Do you agree that leading with Day Master psychological archetypes and life timing feels higher-trust than a generic 8-system astrology portal?"
  3. Light Mode Appeal: "Does having a dedicated Xuan Paper light mode make the product feel more like an editorial self-development tool (like MasterClass or Kinfolk)?"
