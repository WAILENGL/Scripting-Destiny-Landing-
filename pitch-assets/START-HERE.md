# Scripting Destiny — pitch-deck assets

Everything here matches the current dark editorial landing page. Start with Brand-Guide.pdf.

- **01-Logos**: official transparent silver logo and original background logo, exported as true PNGs. No redraws or recolouring.
- **02-Backgrounds**: 1920 × 1080 (16:9) ink-grain and left/right enso backgrounds, no baked-in text.
- **03-Graphics**: transparent enso overlay derived from the existing landing artwork; use at 40–55% opacity on ink. Exact ink divider as SVG and PNG; five-element palette.
- **04-Fonts**: Cormorant Garamond and Karla variable TTF fonts, regular and italic, with SIL Open Font Licences. Install before editing a deck.
- **05-Mobile-Screenshots**: real 393 × 815 mobile views captured at 3× resolution, plus transparent generic phone frames. A deck-ready 16:9 overview is included. Screenshots taken on 22 October 2026. No invented UI or user data.
- **Brand-Colours.json**: CSS source colours and their browser-converted sRGB HEX equivalents. Metallic text is a sheen; solid gold is the editable-slide fallback.

## Slide typography
Cormorant Garamond 300 for large headlines; 500 for smaller section headings. Karla 400 for body copy, 500 for labels, 600 for calls to action. At 16:9: try 44–60 pt titles and 20–24 pt body. Use a quiet ink background, generous margins and gold sparingly. Keep Chinese characters only when they belong to a real chart; no decorative character overlays.

## Brand purpose — not testimonials
- Cycles: Life moves in seasons. BaZi maps them, so you can tell a winter from a harvest — and stop pushing against the one you’re actually in.
- Understanding: It gives language to what you already sense: how you think, lead and recharge — until knowing yourself stops being vague.
- Alignment: Introspection with a map. Look closely enough at your own nature and the choices get quieter — less friction, more alignment.

Asset resolutions follow the original source quality; resizing the logo or artwork cannot add original detail. No standalone full pitch deck has been written.
